# How to enable "Fulfill online orders" for a location

This makes your store use that location’s inventory for online (web) orders. If it’s off, products can show as “sold out” even when that location has stock.

---

## On desktop (Shopify Admin in a browser)

1. **Open Settings**
   - Log in to your Shopify Admin (e.g. `your-store.myshopify.com/admin`).
   - In the bottom-left corner, click **Settings** (gear icon).

2. **Open Locations**
   - In the left sidebar under **Settings**, click **Locations**.

3. **Open the location you use for online orders**
   - Under **All locations**, click the **name of the location** that holds your inventory (e.g. “Main warehouse”, “Store”, or your primary address).
   - Do **not** click “Add location”; click the name of an existing location.

4. **Turn on fulfillment for online orders**
   - On the location page, find the **Fulfillment** section.
   - Find the option: **“Use inventory at this location to fulfill online orders”**.
   - Turn the **toggle ON** (enabled).
   - Click **Save** at the top right.

---

## On the Shopify mobile app

1. Tap **Store** (or **≡** menu), then **Settings**.
2. Under **Store settings**, tap **Locations**.
3. Under **All locations**, tap the location you use for online orders.
4. In the **Fulfillment** section, turn **ON** the option **“Use inventory at this location to fulfill online orders”**.
5. Tap **Save** or **✓**.

---

## If you only have one location

- That location is usually set to fulfill online orders by default.
- Still check: **Settings → Locations → [your location] → Fulfillment** and ensure **“Use inventory at this location to fulfill online orders”** is **ON**.

---

## Summary

| Where to go | What to do |
|-------------|------------|
| **Settings** (bottom-left in admin) | Click to open store settings |
| **Locations** (under Settings) | Click to see all locations |
| **Click the location name** | Open that location’s details |
| **Fulfillment** section | Find **“Use inventory at this location to fulfill online orders”** |
| **Toggle ON** | Enable it, then **Save** |

After this, that location’s inventory is used for your online store. Together with **“Allow customers to purchase when out of stock”** on each product (Products → [product] → Inventory), your products can stay available for sale.
